Privacy Policy

App: Compress PDF & Image to PDF  •  Last updated: November 2, 2026
In short: all your photos, PDFs and ZIP files are processed on your phone. We do not upload, collect or sell your files or personal data.

1. Who we are

This app is published by Phoenix Flare Apps ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, contact us at phoenixflare.apps@gmail.com.

2. Your files stay on your device

The app lets you compress and edit photos, convert images to PDF, convert PDF pages to images, merge, split and protect PDFs, create ZIP files and extract ZIP files. All of this work happens locally on your phone. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or to any other server.

Results are stored inside the app (in the "History" lists) so you can open or share them again. You can delete them at any time from the History lists or from Settings → Storage, or choose to delete them automatically after 7, 30 or 90 days. Uninstalling the app removes them too.

3. Permissions we use

  • Camera – only when you tap "Take a photo" to scan a page. Photos taken this way stay on your phone.
  • Photos and files – only to open the images, PDFs and ZIP files that you choose with the picker. We do not browse your files on our own.
  • Saving to your gallery – only when you tap a "Save to gallery" button, to store an image you chose.

You can turn any permission off in your phone's Settings → Apps. Some features will then stop working.

4. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not require an account or a login. App settings such as theme, vibration feedback and auto-delete preferences are saved on your device only.

5. Sharing

When you tap "Share", the file is handed to the app you pick in your phone's share sheet (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or Drive). That action is started by you, and what the other app does with the file is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

6. Contacting us by email

If you use "Contact us" or "Report a bug" in the app, your email app opens with a message that already contains the app version and your phone's operating system version, to help us fix problems. You can edit or delete this text before you send it. We use your email only to reply to you.

7. Advertising and third parties

The current version of the app does not show ads and does not use analytics or tracking. If we add advertising in the future, we will update this policy first and describe what data the ad provider may collect.

8. Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

9. Security

Because your files are not sent anywhere, they are protected by your phone's own security. If you protect a PDF with a password, the password is not stored by the app and cannot be recovered by us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
